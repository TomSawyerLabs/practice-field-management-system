# Team 114: NetworkTables drops on the field (2026-10-29)

## Goal

Team 114 reported NetworkTables dropping for about a second at a time while
driving their main robot ("114", no suffix) on the field over house Wi-Fi.
They went back to a direct connection. Find out what went wrong and which leg
of the path drops.

## Environment / context

- DS laptop: `DriverStation eb:a3` (a4:f9:33:ca:eb:a3), 10.55.150.65, legacy
  NI Driver Station, on house Wi-Fi "Tom Sawyer Labs" (VLAN 3 "Public").
- Robot 114: slot1, 10.1.14.2, field radio client 48:DA:35:B0:82:9F at
  -40 to -47 dBm.
- Sessions today: 1:57–2:58 PM, 3:02–3:50 PM, 4:42–5:00 PM PDT.
- Per-run telemetry: `~/practice-field-management-system/recordings/practice-<id>/telemetry.csv`
  on steamboat, about 4 rows/s. Rows come from the robot's status replies
  (UDP 1150) that pFMS sniffs on eno1 (`src/robotPacketCapture.ts`), so a gap
  means steamboat saw no robot replies for that long.

## Correction (2026-10-30)

The thread's first diagnosis, that the 5:14 PM Field E-Stop latched TestBird,

## Findings

1. **Gaps happen only in runs where the robot browned out.** Per-run summary
   (start V, min V, brownout samples, gaps > 0.75 s):
   - 2:44 PM (`144414-ff70`): 12.1 V, min 6.79 V, 13 brownout, 3 gaps (1.12, 0.78, 1.44 s)
   - 2:47 PM (`144734-b23d`): 11.7 V, min 6.70 V, 2 brownout, 2 gaps (0.84, 1.02 s)
   - 3:23 PM (`152331-1cba`): 12.2 V, min 6.61 V, 52 brownout, 7 gaps (0.76–1.62 s)
   - 3:38 PM (`153835-7d4f`): min 6.78 V, 1 brownout, 0 gaps
   - The other 27 runs: no brownout, no gaps, min ≥ 7.55 V.
2. In `152331-1cba` the gaps (3:28:47, 3:29:03, 3:29:17, 3:29:33 PM PDT;
   22:28:47–22:29:33Z) each land next to brownout rows (battery 6.5–7.5 V,
   `brownout=true`, `enabled=false`). The ~15 s spacing matches the timelapse
   tick (`src/fieldTimelapse.ts` `TICK_MS`), but the gaps appear only in
   brownout runs, so the timelapse is unlikely to cause them.
3. The robot stayed on the field radio through those runs (no "dropped off
   the field radio" until battery swaps), so it's not a robot-radio reboot.
4. House Wi-Fi leg looks clean: UniFi shows the laptop on U7-Pro East, 6 GHz
   ch 37/160 MHz, -58 to -63 dBm, no roams or disconnects 1:58–3:52 PM.
5. The 4:42–5:00 PM session had no gaps and no brownouts.
6. Not the cause:
   - The DS's FMS TCP reconnects every ~6 s ("50 rapid reconnects in the last
     5m") are the legacy DS re-handshaking on 1750. NetworkTables is a
     separate TCP connection, routed straight through.
   - No SNAT or TCP-affecting rules on the path.
   - Conntrack is not full (362 / 262144 entries).

## Conclusion (current best explanation)

The robot's network goes quiet for about 1–1.6 s when its battery sags into
brownout under load. That would drop NetworkTables on any connection that
runs while the robot is driven hard. The direct connection seeming better is
unproven: it may only be used with lighter loads.
